# ZEnterprise — Key Differences vs. Personal Account / ZBusiness / Zalo OA

## Key Differences of ZEnterprise

1. **The account belongs to the business, not to an individual** — unlike Personal Accounts and ZBusiness (which, despite the business layer on top, is still fundamentally a personal account), ZEnterprise is owned directly by the business.

2. **Passwords can be changed and account access revoked without losing chat history with customers** — because the account belongs to the business, when an employee leaves or access needs to be revoked, the business can change the password/revoke access while retaining the full customer relationship and conversation history — it doesn't disappear along with the individual staff member, as it would with a personal account.

3. **Messages can be managed and monitored, enabling customer service quality evaluation** — thanks to the official API for chat data, the business can monitor messages and measure service quality (response time, issue detection, etc.) — something not possible with Personal Accounts or ZBusiness, which have no API access.

4. **Reaches customers just like personal messaging, with no need to follow an OA** — unlike Zalo OA (where advisory messages require the customer to follow the OA first), ZEnterprise can message/call immediately just like a personal account, as long as the recipient accepts messages from unknown senders.

## Operations — Steps to Convert from ZBusiness to ZEnterprise

1. **Register and declare the business's list of phone numbers** (suggested: 5–10 numbers)
2. **Change the current personal phone number to one of the business phone numbers** from the list above
3. **Give consent** — confirm agreement to convert the account from ZBusiness to ZEnterprise

Note: the chat API only retrieves conversations **from the conversion point forward** — any reporting/analytics that depends on historical data should be exported/archived before conversion. **Rollback to ZBusiness is not possible** once conversion is complete.
